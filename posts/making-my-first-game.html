Making My First Game!

09/15/2026

Lightbringer

Let's start with the fact that I've dropped the ball a bit with keeping the blog posts updated. Current update as of September 15th, 2026: I've completed module 3 and am working through module 4. But this post is about module 1, "Programming with Godot". I'd dabbled a bit with Godot before starting this journey, but that was mostly just following a tutorial on YouTube multiple times and just kind of figuring it out on my own. At the start of module 1, I played the "Learn GDScript From Zero" interactive lessons by GDQuest, then I followed Brackey's Godot Beginner Tutorial and then this tutorial playlist by HeartBeast.

Then I had to decide what kind of platformer I wanted to make. I had an idea that was much bigger scope than I had the time or skill for, so I decided to make a sort of simple prototype version of that game. The idea was to create a kind of puzzle platformer where the player would navigate through dark levels with only a light to guide them. The light would be the main mechanic, keeping the player safe in one mode, and stunning enemies in another. It would also activate or deactivate different platforms. I decide to name this project Lightbringer. I went to itch.io to get a smattering of free-to-use assets and then got to work.

Throughout the project, I would say the hardest thing was figuring out how to make the light interact correctly with enemies and the environment. I spent alot of time looking up and figuring out signals, when to use Area2D nodes for signals vs other nodes, how to tween a sprite (which wasn't as simple as I had thought it'd be), and I learned alot about making game managers. Overall I had to cut alot of ideas I had for the game simply because I had scoped far too big for what I needed to make. Perhaps in the future I'll come back to this game and improve on it.
